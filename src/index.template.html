The rules of the mela

How to lose gracefully

  1. Pick a target. Cups are kind. Buckets are not.
  2. Aim by moving your mouse or dragging a finger. The golden ring is where a full-power throw will land.
  3. Hold to charge, then release to throw. A quick tap drops short — hold longer to throw farther and hit harder.
  4. You get three balls. Every single piece has to go down to win the teddy.
  5. The shopkeeper and the prize teddy are hittable too. Watch the stall take visible damage.
  6. Sometimes a buffalo wanders onto the lane. Hit it to shoo it — but that ball is spent.
  7. Hit Anna and he will step you back. Farther throws are harder to aim.
  8. If you fail, the crowd laughs. Anna laughs louder. That is the real prize.
  9. After your first ball, Anna offers you madira. It is a trick. Refuse, or drink up and fight the wobble.

The stall is run by Shetty Anna. His commentary is included at no extra cost. Sound can be muted with the speaker button.
